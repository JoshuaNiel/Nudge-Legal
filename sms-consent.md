# SMS Messaging & Consent

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This page describes how Nudge ("the Company", "We", "Us", or "Our") sends SMS text messages, and how recipients give and withdraw their consent to receive them. It exists so that recipients — and mobile carriers — can understand exactly how our messaging program works.

---

## What Nudge Is

Nudge is a personal screen-time accountability iOS application. A Nudge user can invite a friend (an "accountability partner") to receive occasional updates about the user's screen-time progress. Accountability partners do **not** need the Nudge app or an account — they simply receive and reply to text messages.

---

## Who Receives Messages

We only send messages to a phone number when a Nudge user has personally added that number to their account as an accountability partner **and** the owner of that number has explicitly agreed to receive messages (see "How Consent Is Collected" below).

We do not purchase, rent, or otherwise obtain phone numbers from third parties, and we never send unsolicited or marketing messages.

---

## How Consent Is Collected (Double Opt-In)

Consent is collected through a two-step, text-based opt-in:

1. **The user adds a contact.** Inside the Nudge app, a user enters the name and phone number of a friend they want as an accountability partner.
2. **The contact receives a one-time consent request.** We send that number a single SMS asking for permission, for example:

   > "Hey, Josh wants your help being more accountable with their screen time. Can we send you updates on their progress? Reply yes or no. You can opt out at any time."

3. **The contact must reply to opt in.** No further messages are sent unless the recipient replies **YES**. If the recipient replies **NO**, their number is removed and they receive a confirmation that they will not be contacted again. If the recipient never replies, no additional messages are sent.

Only after a recipient replies **YES** do they begin receiving accountability messages.

---

## Types of Messages We Send

- **Consent request** — the one-time opt-in message shown above.
- **Opt-in confirmation** — sent once, after a recipient replies YES:

  > "Thanks for helping Josh with their screen time goals! I'll text you when they need a little encouragement. Reply STOP at any time to opt out."

- **Accountability notifications ("nudges")** — occasional updates about the user's screen-time progress, for example:

  > "Josh has been on their phone for 47 minutes. Reply 1 to send encouragement, Reply 2 to give them a reality check, or reply with your own message."

Recipients may reply to a nudge to send a short message of encouragement or a reality check back to the Nudge user.

---

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on the Nudge user's activity and settings. Volume is low: messages to any single recipient are capped at a maximum of 10 per day.

---

## Opting Out and Getting Help

- **Opt out:** Reply **STOP** at any time to stop all messages. This opt-out is honored permanently.
- **Help:** Reply **HELP** for assistance, or contact us using the details below.

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
